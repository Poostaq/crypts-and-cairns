# Crypts & Cairns Naming Registry

**Purpose:** Keep shipped terminology consistent with the project's fidelity-first naming policy while preventing accidental reuse of source-specific names.

This document is design/legal material and must not be included in a Web export.

## Policy

Generic fantasy vocabulary may remain when it describes a broad genre concept rather than identifying the source. Examples include goblin, knight, mage, adventurer, labyrinth, essence, rift, soul level, rank, and monster.

Source-specific proper nouns, named institutions, protected characters, proprietary creatures, artifacts, locations, dialogue, and plot events require original alternatives. Structural mechanics may remain recognizable when their authored expression is original.

Generic terms become reviewable when combined into a distinctive proper noun or when their surrounding implementation recreates protected expression one-to-one.

## Floor 1 structural policy

The following fidelity choices are approved:

- A luminous first floor.
- Four cardinal routes.
- A central navigation landmark.
- Route-specific creature ecologies.
- Four onward doors.

The following must be original:

- Region names and art.
- Creature designs, histories, skills, and drops.
- Room layouts and traversal challenges.
- Landmark presentation and interaction logic.
- Rift conditions, guardians, puzzles, and rewards.
- Dialogue and encounter sequences.

## Release checks

1. Scan shipped IDs, filenames, UI strings, localization, and the export manifest for prohibited terms.
2. Manually review one-to-one mappings that text scanning cannot identify.
3. Verify that this registry and all source-reference documents are excluded from the export.
4. Run marketplace and trademark searches for the game title, city name, institutions, signature artifacts, and major creature names.
